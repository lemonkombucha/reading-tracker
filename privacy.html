Marginalia

Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

What Marginalia is

Marginalia is a personal reading tracker. It is a static website with no server of its own, no accounts database, no analytics and no advertising.

Data you enter

Books, reading sessions, reviews, quotes and settings are stored in your own browser's local storage. They are not sent to the site's author or to anyone else.

Sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, the site asks for one permission: access to its own hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). It uses that only to save and load a single file containing your reading library, so your library is available on your other devices. The site cannot see, read or change any other file in your Google Drive. It also reads your basic Google profile (name, email address and profile picture) only to show them to you on the Settings page; they are kept only in your own browser so you stay signed in, and are not sent anywhere. The short-lived access token is kept only in your own browser and is sent only to Google.

You can remove the site's access and its stored file at any time from your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps and services, or in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

Other services

Book search and cover images are loaded from Open Library, the sign-in script from Google, and the heading font from Google Fonts. Those providers receive the request your browser makes (including your IP address), as with any website. Search terms you type in "Add a book" are sent to Open Library.

Deleting your data

Clear this site's data in your browser. If you signed in with Google, also remove the app as described above.
